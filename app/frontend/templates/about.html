What is GenomicIR-NLP?

GenomicIR-NLP is a research workbench that applies modern NLP techniques to the genomics literature scraped from bioRxiv's genomics collection. It offers three complementary services:

  • Information Retrieval — BM25 ranking across thousands of abstracts.
  • Summarization — extractive sentence scoring based on term frequency.
  • Question Answering — retrieval-augmented sentence extraction grounded in the indexed corpus.

Architecture

Flask (Python) ──HTTP──▶ Fastify (TypeScript) ──▶ CSV index (bioRxiv)

The frontend is intentionally light — no heavyweight JS framework — so the genomics-themed animations (the DNA helix in the background) stay center stage.
